What membren keeps

The part a photo can’t hold.

Not a polished life story. Just the words you would want to find again when the details have started to fade.

01

Your words

One line, a little more, or a whole thought. Write what this moment meant to you, not what would look good to anyone else.

02

The feeling

Keep the emotion alongside it. Sometimes a few words and a feeling say everything you need.

03

The moment around it

Add a photo, a date, or the people who were there when you have them. A memory can be small and still be worth keeping.

Start with a moment from today—or one you’ve been meaning to put into words for years.

For your future self

Come back to more than a photograph.

Your moments gather in a simple timeline. Later, you can find the words you chose, the feeling you named, and the person you were in that moment.

Some dates bring a memory back into view. No promise to recreate the past—just a way back to what you actually wrote.
