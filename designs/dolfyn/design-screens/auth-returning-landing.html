Why simple beats complex

Every note tool eventually asks you to organize before it helps you retrieve. Folders, tags, object types — they're all scaffolding for the tool's sake, not yours.

The alternative is radical: write freely, let the AI build the connections invisibly. No taxonomy, no maintenance, no guilt about unfiled notes.
