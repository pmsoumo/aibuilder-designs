Collapsed state

State E — collapsed summarization card

User tapped the collapse button (chevron) on the summarization card. The card collapses to show only the headline — themes, footer, and cached label are hidden. The card becomes compact and tappable — tapping it expands back to the full view. The chevron rotates 180° to indicate expand. This keeps the chat distraction-free while preserving the summary as a quick-glance reference.
