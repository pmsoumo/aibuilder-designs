FAQ

Every question you might have.

Will the AI actually find my stuff?
Try it free before paying. The free tier includes full AI — 42K tokens/day across chat and inline tools. If it can't find your notes, you owe us nothing.
Will the AI's edits sound like me?
Improve Writing matches your voice from your own notes; Change Tone lets you pick Professional, Casual, Excited, or Assertive. You review every change before it's inserted — replace, insert, or discard.
Are my notes private? Where is my data?
Your notes are private by default. Public sharing is opt-in per note. No lock-in, export anytime — your notes, your data.
What happens when I hit the daily cap?
Nothing is lost. Auto-save keeps every conversation, and the meter is qualitative — Plenty / Halfway / Low — so you always know where you stand. Paid tiers raise the cap to 200K–600K tokens/day.
Is ₹149/mo worth it?
It's 5x cheaper than the next paid competitor, with AI included on every tier. ₹99 for your first 3 months, cancel anytime, no questions.
What about my 1,000+ notes in Notion/Obsidian?
Import in one click. Your system stays in your old tool if you want it to — dolfyn just stops making you organize the new ones.
Do I need to learn a system?
No. There are no folders, tags, or object types. You already know how to write — that's the entire point.
How much can I write on the free tier?
The free tier holds 10 notes with generous space in each — enough for real, long-form thinking. If you outgrow it, Think gives you unlimited notes and unlimited space per note.
