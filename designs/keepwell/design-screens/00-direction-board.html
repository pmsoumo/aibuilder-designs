KeepWell — three directions

Who it's for: a doctor with a patient in the chair who has ten seconds to know who this person is medically.

Owns: composed, exact, human. Core loop: book → read the record → complete the visit → follow-up goes out → patient returns to a richer record.

Each panel renders the same Patient Detail header with the same real content. Judge the feel, not the layout — layout comes next.
