Today — the day and the waiting room on one screen

The queue used to be its own destination, labelled "Patients", which is why a booked patient could never be found there. It is folded in here instead: a solo doctor does not context-switch between "my day" and "my waiting room" — they are the same thing, looked at in the same glance. The order top to bottom is the order attention is owed: someone waiting to be admitted, someone in the room, someone waiting, someone due later.

Desktop — a mid-morning clinic

Two patients checked themselves in from the door QR. They hold no token and no place in the queue until the doctor accepts them — nothing a stranger types enters the record on its own. Accepting issues the token, so the leading cell keeps the token's exact footprint and the column never shifts. Call next is the one primary action on the screen.
