New appointment — typed while someone waits

The highest-friction moment in the product: she is on the phone or facing a patient, and every extra field is a reason to go back to paper. Phone number first, because it is the only field the product genuinely needs — and typing a known number surfaces the existing patient instead of silently creating a duplicate. Day and time are chips, not pickers; a date picker is three taps for "today".

Desktop — entered at the desk

Two columns on desktop — phone beside name, day beside time — so the whole form fits without scrolling. It is typed while a patient waits, and a scroll on a four-field form is wasted motion. Phone stays single-column.
