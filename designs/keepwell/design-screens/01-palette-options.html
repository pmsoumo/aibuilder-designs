Green, three ways — on the real Patient Detail

Each frame is the live Patient Detail screen with only the colour tokens swapped. Rule in all three: one green family + neutrals; "changed since last visit" is a green tint, not yellow; red is reserved for clinical risk. Scroll inside each frame; resize the window for phone width.

1 · Neem Recommended

Deep botanical green on a faintly green-tinted ground. Calm and trustworthy, dark enough to feel exact rather than "wellness". Reads like a good hospital, not a juice bar.

2 · Emerald

Brighter, cleaner emerald on pure white. The most "clinical-modern" and energetic of the three; closest to the category default, so it leans hardest on type and spacing for distinction.

3 · Sage

Muted grey-green, softest and most human. Gentle when the screen is turned toward a patient; risk is that it reads quiet or low-energy at a busy front desk.
