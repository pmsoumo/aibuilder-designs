Patients — the directory that was scoped and never built

Every booking and every accepted check-in already creates a patient row; the app simply never showed them, and the nav item labelled "Patients" rendered the live queue instead. This is that screen. The directory is a rail, not a page, because a name is chosen in order to look at the person — the two are one act, and making it two navigations costs a tap the doctor pays for every patient of the day.

Desktop — list rail beside the record

The vitals strip carries the change, not just the reading. A BP of 138/90 is a fact the doctor already had on paper; "138/90, up from 128/84 in March" is the thing paper never gave them, and it is the single most defensible reason this record exists. The timeline merges the next appointment into the same list as past visits, so history and what's coming read as one thread.
